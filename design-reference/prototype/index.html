What this is

The original design prototypes, wired together so you can walk the real flows. Nothing here is app code — it's the visual source of truth.

Screen changes use the designed ripple: a circular reveal easing out from where you tapped, three trailing gold rings, and the outgoing screen blurring back.

Six screens animate themselves on entry (Settle up, Settings, Tip jar, Help, About, Add friend), so those swap instantly and play their own ripple instead of two at once.

Taps that go nowhere

Are usually screens that were never designed — expense detail, edit expense, date and payer pickers, group settings, and every empty and error state. See plan/phase-10-states-polish.md.
