Water-ripple screen change Every screen change that adds or leaves context — the FAB, the picker's Continue, the back chevron — reveals the next screen through a circular ripple that starts at the tap point. Gold-leaf rings ride the wavefront; the screen being left blurs and eases back a touch, so it reads as dissolving under water rather than sliding away. Roughly 950ms, and it collapses to an instant swap under reduce-motion.

For engineering The effect ships as assets/hisaab-ripple.js — call HisaabRipple.play({ incoming, outgoing, origin, stage }) with the tap point from HisaabRipple.originFromEvent(e, stage). It returns a promise that resolves when the ripple has covered the screen, and no-ops to an instant swap when reduce-motion is set.
